# Title: Multi-Level Order-Flow Imbalance in a Limit Order Book

**Authors:** Authors:Ke Xu, Martin D. Gould, Sam D. Howison
**arXiv:** 1907.06230

## Abstract

Abstract:We study the multi-level order-flow imbalance (MLOFI), which is a vector quantity that measures the net flow of buy and sell orders at different price levels in a limit order book (LOB). Using a recent, high-quality data set for 6 liquid stocks on Nasdaq, we fit a simple, linear relationship between MLOFI and the contemporaneous change in mid-price. For all 6 stocks that we study, we find that the out-of-sample goodness-of-fit of the relationship improves with each additional price level that we include in the MLOFI vector. Our results underline how order-flow activity deep into the LOB can influence the price-formation process.
